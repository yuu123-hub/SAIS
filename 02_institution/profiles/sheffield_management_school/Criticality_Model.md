# Criticality Model

Use this as a diagnostic lens, not a universal grade calculator.

| Level | Observable behaviour | Typical next move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 — Description | defines, reports, or summarises | ask “so what?” and identify a claim |
| 1 — Comparison | contrasts sources or cases | explain why the difference matters |
| 2 — Evaluation | judges strength, fit, evidence, or limitation | test an alternative explanation |
| 3 — Critique | exposes assumptions, boundaries, trade-offs, and competing accounts | make a qualified independent judgement |
| 4 — Synthesis | builds a justified interpretation across evidence and theory | state contribution and limits |

Do not claim a specific threshold corresponds to Distinction unless the current rubric states it.
