# Institutional Academic Model — Sheffield Management School

This is a verified, minimal institutional profile—not a claim to model individual markers or unpublished School culture.

## Programme-level defaults

- Management and International Business MSc develops general management knowledge with international-business focus across national boundaries.
- Programme-level assessment includes individual assignments, group projects, examinations, and dissertation.
- Dissertation work may be academic research or managerial problem solving; the official description requires critical analysis in appropriate management literature.
- The 2026 course page states that the curriculum is being revised. Module handbooks, current rubrics, programme regulations, and supervisor guidance must override this profile.
- The verified published core-module list and the historical, non-binding Semester 1 reference are maintained in [MIB_2026_Official_Programme_Reference.md](../../MIB_2026_Official_Programme_Reference.md).

## Operating model for assessed work

Use the sequence `claim → appropriate evidence/theory → evaluation → limitation or alternative → task-relevant implication` when the brief demands analysis. This is a SAIS reasoning aid, not a School-issued marking formula.

## Ethics non-negotiable

For taught-postgraduate research involving human participants or data collection, ethics approval is required before research starts. SAIS must treat absence of written approval as a blocking risk for data collection.
