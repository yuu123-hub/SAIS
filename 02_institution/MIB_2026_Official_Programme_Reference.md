# Management and International Business MSc — Official Programme Reference

**Profile status:** Verified programme-level reference; current as checked on 24 July 2026.  
**Authority:** University of Sheffield official course page.  
**Use in SAIS:** Programme context only. A current module handbook, module profile, assessment brief, rubric, and lecturer/supervisor guidance override this document.

## Critical status note

The 2026-27 course page states that the curriculum is being revised and that core and optional module details are still being confirmed. Do **not** infer final term allocation, module code, credits, learning outcomes, assessment format, reading lists, or marking criteria from this reference.

## 2026-27 programme facts

- Programme: Management and International Business MSc.
- School: Management School, Faculty of Social Sciences.
- Duration and mode: one year, full-time.
- Start date shown: September 2026.
- Programme emphasis: general management principles with an international-business focus across national boundaries; theory and practical skills.
- Published assessment modes: individual assignments, group projects, end-of-semester examinations, and dissertation.
- Teaching modes listed: lectures, seminars, case studies, collaborative group work, and web-based discussion groups.
- A company-based dissertation with an external organisation is described as a possible route, subject to the University's current arrangements and approval.

## First-semester reference — historical / not confirmed for 2026-27

An official University of Sheffield programme specification indexed as *Programme Specification: Management (International Business) (MSc), MGTT100* describes an earlier structure in which **Semester 1 focused on basic business management modules worth 60 credits in total**. Its indexed text names:

1. Accounting and Financial Management
2. Marketing
3. Operations and Supply Chain Management
4. Managing People in Organisations
5. International Business

This supports a **provisional pre-arrival focus**, not a final 2026-27 timetable. The current 2026 course page explicitly says that the curriculum is under revision. Do not use this list to assume current credits, sequencing, assessments, or enrolment requirements.
