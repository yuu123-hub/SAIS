# University of Sheffield — Management and International Business MSc Profile

## Verified programme-level facts

The University’s 2026 course page describes a one-year full-time MSc combining management theory with practical skills in international business. Listed assessment modes are individual assignments, group projects, end-of-semester examinations, and a dissertation. The dissertation is 45 credits and may take the form of academic research or managerial problem solving; either route requires critical analysis within appropriate management literature.

The 2026 page says that the curriculum is being revised. Its currently published module list and credits may therefore change and must not be treated as a final timetable or used to infer learning outcomes, assessment weights, marking criteria, or detailed requirements. These must be imported from the current official programme regulations, module handbook, assessment brief, rubric, and supervisor guidance.

## Operational implications for SAIS

- Route each assignment through its own brief and rubric, not a generic programme assumption.
- For international-business analysis, consider context across organisations, industries, institutions, countries, and cross-cultural settings when relevant to the question.
- For a dissertation, select the academic-research or managerial-problem-solving route explicitly before developing methods or outputs.
