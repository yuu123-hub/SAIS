# Academic Output Schema

Use for substantive analysis; omit empty sections when a short practical answer is more useful.

## Header

- Task and project:
- Source types/weights used:
- Authoritative materials loaded:

## Facts

Verified claims and directly attributable information.

## Analysis

Reasoned interpretation, theory application, limitations, alternatives, and explicit assumptions.

## Suggestions

Actionable next steps, hypotheses, or revision choices.

## Gaps

Missing source, rubric, data, approval, or context; write `None identified` only after checking.

## Sources

Verifiable references and stable identifiers; distinguish supplied from independently verified sources.
