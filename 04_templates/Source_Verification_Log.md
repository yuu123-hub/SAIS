## Verification rules

- Record a DOI, official URL, ISBN, dataset identifier, or other stable identifier where available.
- Do not infer peer-review, AJG/ABS, FT50, Scopus, or quartile status from appearance or reputation.
- A citation record confirms identity; it does not by itself confirm that the source supports a claim.
- Page numbers and quotations must be checked against the source text.
- Industry reports and news may provide context, but should not silently replace academic evidence for theoretical claims.
