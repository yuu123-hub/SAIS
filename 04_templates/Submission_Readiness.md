## Gate 1: Task alignment

- [ ] The response answers the exact command words and scope.
- [ ] Every rubric criterion has visible evidence in the work.
- [ ] Word/time limits and required sections are satisfied.
- [ ] Lecturer or supervisor directions have been followed or a conflict is recorded.

## Gate 2: Evidence and referencing

- [ ] Material factual claims have traceable support.
- [ ] Quotations and page numbers were checked against originals.
- [ ] References cited in text appear in the reference list and vice versa.
- [ ] Source quality and limitations are appropriate to each claim.
- [ ] No source, ranking, DOI, data point, or quotation was invented.

## Gate 3: Reasoning and criticality

- [ ] The work evaluates rather than only describes where required.
- [ ] Important assumptions and alternative explanations are addressed.
- [ ] Causal language matches the research design and evidence.
- [ ] Limitations and boundary conditions are acknowledged.
- [ ] The conclusion follows from the analysis and does not introduce new claims.

## Gate 4: Communication

- [ ] Structure and signposting make the argument easy to follow.
- [ ] British English and required academic style are consistent.
- [ ] Tables, figures, appendices, and headings are labelled correctly.
- [ ] Proofreading has not changed the intended meaning.

## Gate 5: Integrity and AI use

- [ ] The assessment-specific AI policy was checked.
- [ ] Any permitted AI use stays within that policy.
- [ ] Required acknowledgement or evidence of process is complete.
- [ ] The student can explain and defend every submitted claim and wording choice.
- [ ] Confidential or personal material is handled according to policy.

## Gate 6: Administrative check

- [ ] Candidate details, file name, cover sheet, and submission portal requirements are correct.
- [ ] The final file opens correctly and contains the intended version.
- [ ] A local backup exists.
- [ ] Submission confirmation will be retained.
