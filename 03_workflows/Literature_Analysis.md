# Workflow: Literature Analysis

## Task Context

Article/book/report and the research or assignment question it may inform.

## Required Inputs

Full text or accessible excerpt; bibliographic metadata; a stated purpose for reading if possible.

## Method

1. Capture citation metadata and source type.
2. Separate author claims, methods, results, and limitations.
3. Identify theoretical assumptions and competing explanations.
4. Assess relevance to the current project; do not confuse the paper's argument with your own.
5. Write a reusable reading note and identify the next evidence gap.

## Output

Facts, analysis, useful evidence, limitations, relevance, gaps, and source details.
