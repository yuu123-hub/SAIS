# Workflow: Assessment and Distinction Review

## Required inputs

Draft or plan; current brief; rubric or marking criteria; source list. Without a rubric, review against general postgraduate standards and explicitly label this limitation.

## Method

1. Map each criterion to evidence in the draft or plan.
2. Diagnose description, theory application, evidence quality, argument coherence, independent judgment, structure, and task-relevant implications.
3. Identify the one to three highest-impact changes likely to improve the work.
4. Do not invent a numerical grade. Give a rubric-qualified readiness judgement: `not assessable`, `below stated standard`, `approaching stated standard`, or `evidence of stated standard`.

## Output

Strengths; criterion-by-criterion gaps; priority revision plan; source risks; next action.
