# Workflow: Presentation

## Task Context

Presentation brief, assessment rubric, audience, time limit, required format, and project context.

## Required Inputs

The presentation brief and timing. If the audience, marking criteria, or required content are missing, list the gap before prescribing slide count or style.

## Method

1. Extract the assessed learning outcomes and the intended decision or argument.
2. Design a narrative: problem/question → evidence and analysis → alternative/limitation → conclusion and managerial implication.
3. Allocate time before creating slides; prioritise the evidence necessary for the rubric.
4. Audit claims, source attribution, visual accessibility, and speaker-note assumptions.
5. Create a rehearsal plan with likely questions and concise evidence-based answers.

## Output

Presentation narrative, time budget, slide outline, source plan, challenge questions, gaps, and next action.

## Gaps

Record missing rubric, audience, time limit, format, source permission, accessibility requirement, or rehearsal evidence.

## Next Action

Confirm the missing constraint with the largest design impact or rehearse the highest-risk section to time.
