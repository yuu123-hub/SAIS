# Workflow: Exam Revision and Reflective Work

## Exam revision

Use official learning outcomes and past/authorised practice material. Build concept comparisons, retrieval questions, timed-answer plans, and error logs; do not claim that predicted questions will appear.

## Reflective work

Differentiate experience, interpretation, learning, evidence where required, and future action. Do not force unsupported external citations into a personal reflection, but follow the actual brief and referencing rules.
