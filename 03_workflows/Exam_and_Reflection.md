# Workflow: Exam Revision and Reflective Work

## Task Context

Exam revision or reflective assessment, with official learning outcomes, authorised practice materials, brief, and timing constraints.

## Required Inputs

For exam revision, obtain learning outcomes and authorised scope. For reflective work, obtain the prompt, required model/structure, experience boundaries, and referencing rule.

## Method

### Exam revision

Use official learning outcomes and past/authorised practice material. Build concept comparisons, retrieval questions, timed-answer plans, and error logs; do not claim that predicted questions will appear.

### Reflective work

Differentiate experience, interpretation, learning, evidence where required, and future action. Do not force unsupported external citations into a personal reflection, but follow the actual brief and referencing rules.

## Output

For exams: topic map, retrieval questions, timed plan, and error log. For reflection: experience–interpretation–learning–action map aligned to the brief.

## Gaps

Record missing scope, unauthorised past material, unclear reflective requirements, absent personal evidence, or unknown referencing expectations.

## Next Action

Complete one bounded retrieval practice or draft one student-owned reflective incident analysis.
