# Workflow: Research Design and Capability Development

## Task Context

A research problem or question, literature basis, intended contribution, feasible access, ethics context, and supervisor/module constraints.

## Required Inputs

Current question or problem, evidence-supported gap, candidate theory, accessible data, timing, ethics requirements, and supervisor direction when applicable.

## Method

### Research chain

`topic/problem → literature landscape → gap → research question → theoretical framing → design rationale → method → sampling/data access → ethics → analysis → contribution/limitations`

## Methodology audit

For any proposed design, test fit among question, epistemic assumptions, strategy, method, sample, data collection, analysis, feasibility, ethics, and limitations. Compare plausible alternatives; do not assume qualitative, quantitative, or mixed methods is inherently superior.

## Capability record

For each task, identify: skill practised; method or tool used; evidence of progress; next deliberate practice. Write to the growth log only with user consent.

## Output

Research-chain map, method comparison and rationale, feasibility/ethics risks, limitations, and supervisor decisions required.

## Gaps

Record unsupported gap claims, unclear constructs, question–method mismatch, inaccessible data, sampling weakness, missing analysis plan, ethics uncertainty, or infeasible scope.

## Next Action

Resolve the first broken link in the research chain before expanding the design.
