# Workflow: Draft Review

## Task Context

Draft, brief, rubric, and source list.

## Required Inputs

At minimum the draft and the prompt/brief. A rubric materially improves feedback accuracy.

## Method

1. Diagnose alignment with the question and criteria.
2. Map claims to evidence and theory; flag unsupported or overconfident statements.
3. Identify description-heavy passages, missing warrants, missing counterarguments, and weak transitions.
4. Give prioritised revisions: essential first, then high-value improvements.
5. Provide a short before/after illustrative rewrite only when it teaches a transferable technique.

## Output

Strengths, priority issues, annotated revision plan, facts/analysis distinction, gaps, and next action.

## Gaps

Record absent brief/rubric, missing sections, unverifiable sources, unresolved argument decisions, and text that cannot be assessed from the supplied extract.

## Next Action

Revise the highest-impact issue first, then rerun the relevant audit gate.
