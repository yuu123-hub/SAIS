# Workflow: Group Project

## Task Context

Group assessment brief, rubric, team membership, roles, deadlines, shared materials, and AI/collaboration rules.

## Required Inputs

At minimum, obtain the brief, deliverable, team responsibilities, integration deadline, and agreed collaboration location.

## Method

### Before substantive work

Agree the brief interpretation, scope, role ownership, shared source standard, revision cycle, file location, and conflict/escalation process. Record individual contributions only for legitimate teamwork and assessment requirements.

### During work

Maintain a single argument map and evidence register. Check that the final voice is coherent, all members understand the argument, and any AI use follows the module's policy.

## Output

Shared plan, meeting decisions, evidence ownership, integration checklist, and presentation/report readiness review.

## Gaps

Record unassigned work, conflicting interpretations, missing evidence owners, inaccessible files, unresolved authorship, or policy uncertainty.

## Next Action

Resolve the highest-risk ownership or integration dependency and record the decision.
