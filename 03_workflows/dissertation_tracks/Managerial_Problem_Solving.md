# Dissertation Track: Managerial Problem Solving

## Use when

The primary purpose is to analyse a defined management problem and produce evidence-led, context-sensitive recommendations.

## Required decisions

1. Client/stakeholder and decision problem
2. Scope, context, constraints, and success criteria
3. Relevant management literature and theoretical framing
4. Evidence plan and research design
5. Ethics, access, confidentiality, and conflicts of interest before data collection
6. Analysis, options, recommendation, implementation implications, risks, and evaluation plan

## Quality checks

- Do not confuse a client preference with a research finding.
- Compare feasible alternatives and their trade-offs.
- Keep recommendations proportionate to available evidence.
- Separate commercially confidential material into ignored project folders.
