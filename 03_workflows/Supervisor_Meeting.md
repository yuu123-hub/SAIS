# Workflow: Supervisor Meeting and Feedback

## Task Context

Previous feedback, current draft/status, meeting date, and questions.

## Method

1. Separate direct supervisor feedback from your interpretation.
2. Convert each item into a decision, action, owner, due date, and evidence of completion.
3. Identify contradictions with existing decisions or governance rules.
4. Prepare a concise agenda with the highest-impact unresolved questions first.

## Output

Meeting agenda or feedback action log, dependencies, unresolved decisions, and next action.
