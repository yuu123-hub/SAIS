- SAIS MVP Version: 0.1.0

- Purpose: Test research-design fit before data collection or strong methodological claims.
- Applicable Tasks: Methodology planning; proposal planning; dissertation design
- Authority Level: Workflow
- Known Gaps: Current brief, ethics requirements and supervisor direction are required before recommending data collection.

## Research chain

`topic/problem -> literature landscape -> gap -> research question -> theoretical framing -> design rationale -> method -> sampling/data access -> ethics -> analysis -> contribution/limitations`

Resolve the earliest broken link before elaborating later steps.

## Methodology audit

Test fit among question, epistemic assumptions where relevant, strategy, method, sample, data collection, analysis, feasibility, ethics and limitations. Compare plausible alternatives; qualitative, quantitative and mixed methods are not inherently superior. Do not treat correlation as causation without a defensible design.

## Output

Produce a research-chain map, alternatives and rationale, feasibility/ethics risks, limitations, questions for a supervisor, and a smallest next action. Do not approve data collection or claim ethics approval without the relevant current evidence.

# Dissertation Workflow

- SAIS MVP Version: 0.1.0
- Document Status: Canonical-source export
- Document Type: Workflow
- Target Institution: The University of Sheffield
- Target Programme: Management and International Business MSc
- Source Files: `03_workflows/Dissertation.md`; `dissertation_tracks/Academic_Research.md`; `dissertation_tracks/Managerial_Problem_Solving.md`; `04_templates/Dissertation_Status.md`
- Source Versions: SAIS v1.6.0
- Purpose: Plan a dissertation through a traceable research or managerial-problem route.
- Applicable Tasks: Dissertation idea; proposal; research design; milestone review
- Authority Level: Workflow
- Known Gaps: Programme rules, supervisor direction and ethics approval remain task-time requirements.

## Choose one route

### Academic Research

Use when the primary purpose is a scholarly research question and qualified academic contribution. Decide topic/problem, literature gap/question, theory and alternatives, design/method/sampling/data/analysis, ethics, then findings/discussion/contribution/limits.

### Managerial Problem Solving

Use when the primary purpose is a defined management problem and evidence-led recommendation. Decide stakeholder/problem, scope/success criteria, literature/theory, evidence/design, ethics/access/confidentiality, analysis/options/recommendation/implementation risk.

Do not treat the routes as interchangeable. A client preference is not research evidence; an academic contribution is not automatically a managerial recommendation.

## Core checks

- The question is answerable with the proposed evidence.
- The method follows the question and stated assumptions.
- Literature review synthesises debates rather than listing papers.
- Ethics, access, confidentiality and timeline are feasible before data collection.
- Findings, interpretation and contribution remain distinct.
