# Assignment Brief Analysis Workflow

- SAIS MVP Version: 0.1.0
- Document Status: Canonical-source export
- Document Type: Workflow
- Target Institution: The University of Sheffield
- Target Programme: Management and International Business MSc
- Source Files: `03_workflows/Assignment_Analysis.md`; `04_templates/Assignment_Brief_Analysis.md`; `03_workflows/Assessment_Review.md`
- Source Versions: SAIS v1.6.0
- Purpose: Convert a current brief and rubric into a bounded, evidence-led plan.
- Applicable Tasks: Assignment brief analysis; essay/report/proposal planning
- Authority Level: Workflow; subordinate to current official materials
- Known Gaps: Do not infer missing rubric, learning outcomes, AI policy or lecturer direction.

## Required inputs

Brief and rubric are required for a complete analysis. Also request learning outcomes, deadline, word/time limit, assessment type, source constraints, referencing rule, and assessment-specific AI-use policy. If something is absent, label it as required now, needed before drafting, or needed before submission.

## Method

1. Extract the deliverable, command words, scope/exclusions, administrative constraints and required evidence.
2. Convert every material rubric criterion into a visible success condition.
3. Identify concepts, theories, cases and evidence categories without preselecting a conclusion.
4. Propose research questions, a research plan and a provisional outline with an appropriate counterargument or limitation move.
5. Record decisions that require lecturer clarification.

## Output

Use `templates/Essay_Template.md`, `templates/Report_Template.md` or `templates/Proposal_Template.md` as appropriate. Return facts extracted from the brief, analysis, a staged plan, gaps and the smallest next action.

## Quality check

Do not claim that a generic MIB or UK MSc expectation overrides a current brief. Do not draft final assessed prose before the AI-use boundary is known.

# Essay Workflow

- SAIS MVP Version: 0.1.0
- Document Status: Export-only attributed composite
- Document Type: Essay planning, drafting-support, revision and audit workflow
- Target Institution: The University of Sheffield
- Target Programme: Management and International Business MSc
- Source Files: `Assignment_Analysis.md`; `Project_Lifecycle.md`; `Draft_Review.md`; `Assessment_Review.md`; `Argument_Map.md`; `Paragraph_Argument.md`
- Source Versions: SAIS v1.6.0
- Purpose: Provide a rubric-led composite route for essays.
- Applicable Tasks: Essay planning; drafting support; revision; audit
- Authority Level: Workflow; subordinate to current brief/rubric and AI-use rule
- Known Gaps: No original standalone Essay Workflow or Essay Template exists in the repository.

> Export-only attributed composite; not an original Canonical module.

## Stages

1. **Interpret**: apply Assignment Brief Analysis; identify command words, scope, criteria and evidence needs.
2. **Research**: build a bounded evidence base; create source notes and map evidence to possible claims.
3. **Design the argument**: state a provisional thesis; for each major claim identify theory, evidence, warrant, counter-evidence, limitation and implication.
4. **Draft with the applicable AI boundary**: student authorship and verified sources remain central. Do not use direct AI output as submission text.
5. **Revise**: diagnose task alignment, evidence fit, description-heavy passages, missing warrants, alternatives and transitions. Prioritise essential fixes.
6. **Audit**: use Submission Audit Workflow and all relevant quality-control files.

## Paragraph diagnostic

Use Claim -> Evidence/Theory -> Analysis -> Link. The analysis should explain why the evidence supports, qualifies or challenges the claim; it must not simply repeat the source.

## Output

Produce a plan, argument map, revision plan or audit rather than a generic essay. Mark unsupported claims and missing inputs as gaps.

# Report Workflow

- SAIS MVP Version: 0.1.0
- Document Status: Export-only attributed composite
- Document Type: Report planning, drafting-support, revision and audit workflow
- Target Institution: The University of Sheffield
- Target Programme: Management and International Business MSc
- Source Files: `Assignment_Analysis.md`; `Management_and_IB_Analysis.md`; `Project_Lifecycle.md`; `Draft_Review.md`; `Assessment_Review.md`; `Argument_Map.md`
- Source Versions: SAIS v1.6.0
- Purpose: Provide a rubric-led composite route for management and international-business reports.
- Applicable Tasks: Report planning; drafting support; revision; audit
- Authority Level: Workflow; subordinate to current brief/rubric and AI-use rule
- Known Gaps: No original standalone Report Workflow or Report Template exists in the repository.

> Export-only attributed composite; not an original Canonical module.

## Method

1. Analyse the current brief and rubric before choosing sections.
2. Define the focal decision, organisation/case, context and time period.
3. Choose only materially relevant levels of analysis: individual, team, organisation, industry/ecosystem, institutions/countries, international/global, and time.
4. Link each major finding or recommendation to theory, case evidence, alternative explanation, limitation and conditional implication.
5. Keep recommendations proportionate to the evidence; do not turn managerial preference into a research finding.
6. Run the revision and submission-audit checks.

## Output

Use `templates/Report_Template.md`. Give a report structure only when it fits the supplied brief. If the brief requires another genre, follow the brief rather than this composite route.
