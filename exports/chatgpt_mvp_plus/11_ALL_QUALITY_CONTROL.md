# Challenger

- SAIS MVP Version: 0.1.0
- Document Status: Canonical-source export
- Document Type: Critical challenge prompt set
- Target Institution: The University of Sheffield
- Target Programme: Management and International Business MSc
- Source Files: `07_quality_control/Challenger.md`
- Source Versions: SAIS v1.6.0
- Purpose: Stress-test an argument, outline, literature synthesis or research design.
- Applicable Tasks: Planning; revision; literature review; methodology; dissertation
- Authority Level: Quality control
- Known Gaps: Challenge questions do not substitute for evidence.

Ask:

1. What hidden assumption must be true?
2. What alternative theory or explanation fits the evidence?
3. What evidence would weaken or falsify the conclusion?
4. Is correlation confused with causation, context with generality, or preference with evidence?
5. What boundary condition, stakeholder, institutional context or cross-cultural factor is overlooked?
6. Which limitation should be acknowledged rather than defended away?
7. What is gained and lost by the chosen framing?
8. Does each conclusion have evidence at the stated strength?
9. Is the managerial implication concrete and conditional rather than generic?
10. What limits transferability?

Return the strongest challenge, evidence need, consequence for the argument and a practical revision option.

- Target Programme: Management and International Business MSc

- Source Versions: SAIS v1.6.0; Harvard guide last reviewed Summer 2025
- Purpose: Check traceability, support and Harvard consistency.
- Applicable Tasks: Draft revision; citation audit; submission audit
- Authority Level: Quality control
- Known Gaps: Consult the current University Library Harvard guide for unlisted source types.

## In-text checks

- [ ] Every material external claim has an appropriate citation or is explicitly a gap/inference.
- [ ] Each source supports the wording and strength of the claim.
- [ ] Direct quotations and specific source details have checked page/location references.
- [ ] Causal claims are not supported by evidence that establishes only association.
- [ ] Secondary citations are necessary, transparent and correctly treated.
- [ ] Statistics, rankings, DOI and peer-review claims are verified, not inferred.

## Reference-list checks

- [ ] Every in-text citation has a matching reference and vice versa.
- [ ] Author, date, title, source/container, publication details and stable identifier are checked.
- [ ] The reference follows the canonical Sheffield Harvard guide for its source type.
- [ ] Unknown details are marked `VERIFY`; no missing detail is invented.
- [ ] The current University Library guide was checked where the source type is unusual or guidance may have changed.

# Submission Checklist

- SAIS MVP Version: 0.1.0
- Document Status: Canonical-source export
- Document Type: Submission-readiness checklist
- Target Institution: The University of Sheffield
- Target Programme: Management and International Business MSc
- Source Files: `04_templates/Submission_Readiness.md`; `03_workflows/Project_Lifecycle.md`
- Source Versions: SAIS v1.6.0
- Purpose: Check task, evidence, reasoning, communication, integrity and administration before submission.
- Applicable Tasks: Final submission audit
- Authority Level: Quality control
- Known Gaps: A ready status is not a prediction of a mark.

## Task alignment

- [ ] Exact command words and scope are answered.
- [ ] Every material rubric criterion has visible evidence or a recorded gap.
- [ ] Word/time limits, required sections and directions are satisfied.

## Evidence and references

- [ ] Material factual claims are traceable.
- [ ] Quotations/pages are checked against originals.
- [ ] In-text citations and reference list match.
- [ ] Source quality and limitations fit each claim.
- [ ] Nothing is invented.

## Reasoning and communication

- [ ] The work evaluates where required.
- [ ] Assumptions, alternatives, causal limits and boundary conditions are addressed.
- [ ] The conclusion follows from the analysis.
- [ ] Structure, signposting, British English, tables/figures and proofreading are appropriate.

## Integrity and administration

- [ ] The assessment-specific AI rule and any declaration are complete.
- [ ] The student can explain and defend the final work.
- [ ] File format, filename, candidate/cover requirements, portal requirements and deadline/timezone are correct.
- [ ] The final file opens and the intended version is retained locally.

Verdict: Not ready / Ready with recorded risks / Ready.
