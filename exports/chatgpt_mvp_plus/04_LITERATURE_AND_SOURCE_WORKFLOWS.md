# Literature Review Workflow

- SAIS MVP Version: 0.1.0
- Document Status: Canonical-source export
- Document Type: Workflow
- Target Institution: The University of Sheffield
- Target Programme: Management and International Business MSc
- Source Files: `03_workflows/Literature_Search_and_Synthesis.md`; `Literature_Input_Pipeline.md`; `04_templates/research/Search_Log.md`; `Literature_Synthesis.md`; `Claim_Evidence_Matrix.md`
- Source Versions: SAIS v1.6.0
- Purpose: Build a transparent, bounded evidence base without overstating coverage or a literature gap.
- Applicable Tasks: Literature review; evidence base; literature gap analysis
- Authority Level: Workflow
- Known Gaps: Database access and full text may be unavailable; web results are provisional unless verified.

## Select a search mode

| Mode | Use | Permitted claim |
|---|---|---|
| Exploratory scan | Learn vocabulary and find leads | Identifies leads; not comprehensive. |
| Structured coursework search | Build a defensible assignment evidence base | Transparent and bounded. |
| Dissertation review | Support gap, theory and design decisions | Comprehensive within documented scope; not automatically systematic. |
| Systematic review | Only when required by the design and reporting standard | Use the required formal protocol. |

## Method

1. Frame the question or decision the search must inform.
2. Define scope: concepts, context, population/organisation, geography, period and source types.
3. Build concept blocks, synonyms, exclusions and reproducible Boolean strings.
4. Choose and justify databases or repositories; log exact searches, filters, dates and counts.
5. Deduplicate and screen against predeclared inclusion/exclusion criteria; record full-text exclusion reasons.
6. Use citation searching carefully and record how sources were found.
7. Verify and extract each included source.
8. Synthesize by theory, context, method, finding, contradiction and limitation rather than author-by-author summary.
9. Map support, challenge, uncertainty and gaps to claims.
10. Apply a stop rule and state limitations.

## Literature-gap rule

Do not state that no research exists because few papers were found. A defensible gap is an unresolved theoretical contradiction, underexamined context/boundary, methodological weakness, inconsistent/insufficient evidence, or practical problem not adequately explained. Prefer `not identified within the documented search` where confidence is limited.

# Source Analysis Workflow

- SAIS MVP Version: 0.1.0
- Document Status: Canonical-source export
- Document Type: Workflow
- Target Institution: The University of Sheffield
- Target Programme: Management and International Business MSc
- Source Files: `03_workflows/Literature_Analysis.md`; `Literature_Input_Pipeline.md`; `04_templates/Reading_Note.md`; `Source_Verification_Log.md`
- Source Versions: SAIS v1.6.0
- Purpose: Analyse one source without confusing its claims with the student's argument.
- Applicable Tasks: Article/PDF/source analysis; reading note; evidence extraction
- Authority Level: Workflow
- Known Gaps: Full text, metadata, pages and legal access may be incomplete.

## Required inputs

The full text or accessible excerpt, bibliographic metadata, legal/access context and the question the source may inform.

## Method

1. Record citation metadata, source type and stable identifier.
2. Separate the author's research question, theory/concepts, method/data, findings and stated limitations.
3. Identify assumptions, competing explanations and defensible additional limitations.
4. Assess relevance to the current task without treating the source's argument as the student's conclusion.
5. Record quotations and pages only when checked against the source.
6. State claims the source may support, challenge or leave unresolved.
7. Create a Reading Note and identify the next evidence or verification gap.

## Output

Return Facts, Analysis, Suggestions, Gaps and Sources. Use AOCOM Lite terms so Author Claim, Evidence, Academic Claim and Inference remain distinct.
