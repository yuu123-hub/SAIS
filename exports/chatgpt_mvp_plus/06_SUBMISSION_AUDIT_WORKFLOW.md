## Required inputs

Final draft or plan; current brief; rubric or criteria; reference list/source records; assessment-specific AI-use rule; relevant instructions; and completed or draft assessment evidence ledger where available.

## Review order

1. **Task alignment**: command words, scope, rubric, word/time limit and required sections.
2. **Evidence and citation**: claim traceability, quotation/page checks, source quality, reference-list matching and no invented details.
3. **Reasoning and criticality**: argument coherence, theory use, alternatives, causal discipline, limitations and conclusion fit.
4. **Communication**: structure, signposting, British English, tables/figures and proofreading.
5. **Integrity and AI**: current policy, declaration, student authorship and confidentiality.
6. **Administration**: format, filename, portal requirements, opening the final file and deadline/timezone.

## Output

Use `Not ready`, `Ready with recorded risks`, or `Ready`; identify blocking issues, residual risks and the next action. Do not invent a mark.
