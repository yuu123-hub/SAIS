## Terms

| Term | Operational meaning | Handling rule |
|---|---|---|
| Source | A supplied or independently verified work, dataset, official document or other identifiable material. | Record type and stable identifier where available. |
| Author Claim | What a source explicitly argues, reports or concludes. | Attribute it; do not treat it as the student's conclusion. |
| Evidence | Information that can support, qualify or challenge a claim. | Check fit, context, method and limitations. |
| Academic Claim | A contestable proposition in the student's argument. | Link to evidence, warrant and limitation. |
| Analysis | Explanation of why evidence matters, including theory, comparison or mechanism. | Do not merely repeat the source. |
| Inference | A bounded interpretation drawn from evidence. | Label as interpretation; do not state as a verified fact. |
| Citation | A traceable reference to the source used. | Verify identity and location; a citation alone is not evidence fit. |
| Limitation | A boundary, alternative explanation, uncertainty or weakness. | State what it changes about the conclusion. |
| Claim-Evidence relationship | The documented connection between a claim and supporting, challenging or missing evidence. | Use the Claim-Evidence Matrix or Assessment Evidence Ledger. |

## Safe sequence

`Source -> Author Claim -> Evidence -> Academic Claim -> Analysis -> Inference -> Limitation -> Citation check`

This is a reasoning aid, not a formal ontology, database schema or lifecycle system. Keep unresolved links in **Known Gaps** rather than filling them with plausible content.
