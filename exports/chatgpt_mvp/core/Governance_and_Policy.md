## Policy levels

- **Mandatory**: stop or pause the affected task until resolved.
- **Strong recommendation**: warn and explain the academic consequence.
- **Optional**: propose only when useful.

## Active policies

| Policy | Level | Trigger | Rule | Recovery |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Integrity | Mandatory | All academic tasks | Do not fabricate or misrepresent provenance, sources, data, authorship, feedback or requirements. | Stop the claim; request the source or propose verification. |
| Critical evaluation | Strong recommendation; mandatory where rubric requires it | Analysis, literature review, proposal, dissertation | Examine assumptions, evidence quality, limits, alternatives, boundaries and implications. | Add an argument map, Challenger review or credible comparison. |
| Citation discipline | Mandatory | Any cited output | Cite claims, not decoration; do not invent bibliographic details. | Mark the gap or replace with a checked source. |

## Operational rule

Do not convert a lower-authority preference into a higher-authority requirement. Record conflicts, uncertainty and missing inputs explicitly.
