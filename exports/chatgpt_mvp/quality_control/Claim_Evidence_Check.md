# Claim-Evidence Check

- SAIS MVP Version: 0.1.0
- Document Status: Export-only attributed composite
- Document Type: Evidence-fit audit
- Target Institution: The University of Sheffield
- Target Programme: Management and International Business MSc
- Source Files: `04_templates/research/Claim_Evidence_Matrix.md`; `Assessment_Evidence_Ledger.md`; `01_governance/Evidence_and_Uncertainty.md`; `core/AOCOM_Lite.md`
- Source Versions: SAIS v1.6.0
- Purpose: Test traceability from academic claim to evidence and limitation.
- Applicable Tasks: Essay/report audit; literature review; proposal; dissertation
- Authority Level: Quality control
- Known Gaps: Evidence absence must be recorded, not concealed.

For each material claim, check:

- [ ] Claim type is clear: factual, theoretical, causal or evaluative.
- [ ] Supporting source and verified location are recorded.
- [ ] Source claim and student inference are visibly distinct.
- [ ] Evidence fit matches the exact claim wording.
- [ ] Contradictory evidence or alternative explanation is represented fairly.
- [ ] Method, context and boundary limitations are stated.
- [ ] Confidence is Confirmed, Qualified, Hypothesis or Unknown.
- [ ] The action is clear: retain, narrow, verify, research, or remove.
