## Checklist

- Does the work answer the stated task and supplied rubric?
- Are material factual claims supported by identifiable sources?
- Are facts, interpretation and recommendations distinct?
- Are citations, quotations, dates and pages verified rather than invented?
- Does the work evaluate rather than merely describe where the task requires criticality?
- Does the evidence support the conclusion at the strength claimed?
- Are alternatives, counter-evidence, limiting conditions and generalisation limits addressed where relevant?
- For MIB work, are relevant theoretical/practical perspectives compared and managerial implications proportionate?
- Are uncertainty and missing information recorded?
- Is language clear and appropriate in British English where requested?
