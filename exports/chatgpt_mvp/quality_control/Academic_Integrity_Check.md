- SAIS MVP Version: 0.1.0
- Document Status: Export-only attributed composite
- Document Type: Integrity and AI-use audit
- Target Institution: The University of Sheffield
- Target Programme: Management and International Business MSc
- Source Files: `01_governance/Academic_Integrity.md`; `AI_Use_in_Assessment.md`; `policies/POL-001_Integrity.md`; `02_institution/Sheffield_GenAI_Reference.md`
- Source Versions: SAIS v1.6.0; Sheffield references checked July 2026
- Purpose: Identify blocking integrity, authorship and AI-use risks.
- Applicable Tasks: All assessed tasks; submission audit
- Authority Level: Mandatory quality control
- Known Gaps: Assessment-specific AI rules must be supplied for a complete check.

- [ ] No source, quotation, page, DOI, result, dataset, feedback item or institutional rule is invented or altered.
- [ ] Facts, source claims, student claims, analysis and inference are distinguishable.
- [ ] The student can explain and defend the argument, evidence choices and final wording.
- [ ] The current assessment-specific AI rule is known and followed.
- [ ] Policy unknown is treated as AI-Free for drafting/translation.
- [ ] Any permitted AI use is substantively reviewed, verified and declared if required.
- [ ] No confidential, personal, employer, interview or unpublished material was used without appropriate authority.
- [ ] Blocking risks are named with a safe next action.
