## Map every material claim

| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| Claim | What exactly is being asserted: factual, theoretical, causal or evaluative? |
| Source and location | Which checked source and page/location supports it? |
| Fit | Does the design/context support this exact wording and strength? |
| Challenge | What evidence or theory complicates it? |
| Limitation | What boundary, method issue or uncertainty applies? |
| Confidence | Confirmed, Qualified, Hypothesis or Unknown? |
| Action | Narrow, remove, verify, find stronger evidence or retain with qualification? |

## Audit questions

- Is a citation being used as evidence rather than decoration?
- Is causal language justified by the design?
- Are context and boundary conditions preserved?
- Is contradictory evidence represented fairly?
- Does the source support the student's exact claim, rather than merely the topic?

Use `templates/Literature_Review_Template.md` and the relevant essay/report template to record the mapping. Do not fill an evidence gap with a plausible citation.
