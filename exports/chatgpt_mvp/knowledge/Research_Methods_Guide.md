## Research chain

`Question -> concept/theory -> design -> data -> analysis -> result -> bounded conclusion`

If a link is weak, the conclusion must be narrower.

## Core distinctions

| Topic | Practical rule |
|---|---|
| Quantitative / qualitative / mixed methods | Choose for fit with the question, not familiarity. Mixed methods require integration, not two disconnected studies. |
| Construct and operationalisation | Check whether an observed measure represents the intended concept rather than a convenient proxy. |
| Sampling | Define population, sample and sampling frame. A large sample cannot repair a biased route or undefined population. |
| Association and causation | Correlation leaves reverse causality, third variables, selection, measurement and chance open unless the design addresses them. |
| Quantitative interpretation | Consider magnitude and uncertainty, not only statistical significance. |
| Qualitative interpretation | Maintain an audit trail, look for negative cases, distinguish participant text/code/theme/inference, and state transferability limits. |
| Quality | Ask about validity, reliability, credibility and transferability in the terms appropriate to the design. |

Before real data analysis, record provenance, consent/ethics status, cleaning choices, missing-data treatment, assumptions, software/version and deviations from plan.
