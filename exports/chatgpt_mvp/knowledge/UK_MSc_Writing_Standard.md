## Standard of work expected by this pack

- Answer the exact question and demonstrate the current rubric, not a generic topic overview.
- Make a precise, contestable claim; support it with appropriate theory and verified evidence.
- Explain the warrant: why the evidence supports, qualifies or challenges the claim.
- Evaluate evidence strength, assumptions, alternative explanations, boundaries and practical implications where relevant.
- Make a proportionate, qualified judgement rather than using stronger language to appear critical.
- Use clear signposting and British academic English. Separate fact, interpretation, suggestion and uncertainty.

## Criticality ladder

| Level | Move |
|---|---|
| Description | State what a source, case or concept says. |
| Comparison | Identify meaningful agreement or difference. |
| Evaluation | Judge fit, evidence quality or limitation under stated conditions. |
| Critique | Test assumptions, trade-offs, boundaries and competing accounts. |
| Synthesis | Build a justified position across evidence and theory, with limits. |

## Paragraph engine

`Claim -> Evidence/Theory -> Analysis -> Link`

Before calling a passage critical, ask: Have I distinguished the source claim from my interpretation? Have I named a limitation, alternative or counter-evidence? Does my judgement follow from evidence? What changes in practice, for whom and under what condition?
