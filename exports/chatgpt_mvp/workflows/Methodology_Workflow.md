## Research chain

`topic/problem -> literature landscape -> gap -> research question -> theoretical framing -> design rationale -> method -> sampling/data access -> ethics -> analysis -> contribution/limitations`

Resolve the earliest broken link before elaborating later steps.

## Methodology audit

Test fit among question, epistemic assumptions where relevant, strategy, method, sample, data collection, analysis, feasibility, ethics and limitations. Compare plausible alternatives; qualitative, quantitative and mixed methods are not inherently superior. Do not treat correlation as causation without a defensible design.

## Output

Produce a research-chain map, alternatives and rationale, feasibility/ethics risks, limitations, questions for a supervisor, and a smallest next action. Do not approve data collection or claim ethics approval without the relevant current evidence.
