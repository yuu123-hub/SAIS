# Source Analysis Workflow

- SAIS MVP Version: 0.1.0
- Document Status: Canonical-source export
- Document Type: Workflow
- Target Institution: The University of Sheffield
- Target Programme: Management and International Business MSc
- Source Files: `03_workflows/Literature_Analysis.md`; `Literature_Input_Pipeline.md`; `04_templates/Reading_Note.md`; `Source_Verification_Log.md`
- Source Versions: SAIS v1.6.0
- Purpose: Analyse one source without confusing its claims with the student's argument.
- Applicable Tasks: Article/PDF/source analysis; reading note; evidence extraction
- Authority Level: Workflow
- Known Gaps: Full text, metadata, pages and legal access may be incomplete.

## Required inputs

The full text or accessible excerpt, bibliographic metadata, legal/access context and the question the source may inform.

## Method

1. Record citation metadata, source type and stable identifier.
2. Separate the author's research question, theory/concepts, method/data, findings and stated limitations.
3. Identify assumptions, competing explanations and defensible additional limitations.
4. Assess relevance to the current task without treating the source's argument as the student's conclusion.
5. Record quotations and pages only when checked against the source.
6. State claims the source may support, challenge or leave unresolved.
7. Create a Reading Note and identify the next evidence or verification gap.

## Output

Return Facts, Analysis, Suggestions, Gaps and Sources. Use AOCOM Lite terms so Author Claim, Evidence, Academic Claim and Inference remain distinct.
