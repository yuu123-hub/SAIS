## Method

1. Analyse the current brief and rubric before choosing sections.
2. Define the focal decision, organisation/case, context and time period.
3. Choose only materially relevant levels of analysis: individual, team, organisation, industry/ecosystem, institutions/countries, international/global, and time.
4. Link each major finding or recommendation to theory, case evidence, alternative explanation, limitation and conditional implication.
5. Keep recommendations proportionate to the evidence; do not turn managerial preference into a research finding.
6. Run the revision and submission-audit checks.

## Output

Use `templates/Report_Template.md`. Give a report structure only when it fits the supplied brief. If the brief requires another genre, follow the brief rather than this composite route.
