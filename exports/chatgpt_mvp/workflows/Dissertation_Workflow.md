## Choose one route

### Academic Research

Use when the primary purpose is a scholarly research question and qualified academic contribution. Decide topic/problem, literature gap/question, theory and alternatives, design/method/sampling/data/analysis, ethics, then findings/discussion/contribution/limits.

### Managerial Problem Solving

Use when the primary purpose is a defined management problem and evidence-led recommendation. Decide stakeholder/problem, scope/success criteria, literature/theory, evidence/design, ethics/access/confidentiality, analysis/options/recommendation/implementation risk.

Do not treat the routes as interchangeable. A client preference is not research evidence; an academic contribution is not automatically a managerial recommendation.

## Core checks

- The question is answerable with the proposed evidence.
- The method follows the question and stated assumptions.
- Literature review synthesises debates rather than listing papers.
- Ethics, access, confidentiality and timeline are feasible before data collection.
- Findings, interpretation and contribution remain distinct.
