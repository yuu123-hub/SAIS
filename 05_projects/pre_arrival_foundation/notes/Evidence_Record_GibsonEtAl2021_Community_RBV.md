# Evidence Record: Gibson et al. (2021) — Community as an RBV resource

- **Citation key / stable identifier:** GibsonEtAl2021; https://doi.org/10.1177/0149206320987289
- **Source weight:** Peer-reviewed theoretical article; high-weight for conceptual framing, not sufficient alone for causal claims.
- **Claim supported or challenged:** Community relationships may be conceptualised as strategic resources through RBV's value, rarity, inimitability, and non-substitutability criteria.
- **Exact finding or quotation (with verified location):** 'the community in which a firm is embedded is a valuable, rare, inimitable, and nonsubstitutable resource' (Gibson, Gibson and Webster, 2021, p. 1878).
- **Context, method, and limitations:** Conceptual article with a literature review and illustrative company comparisons; the appendix reports a search across 85 journals and retention of 25 directly relevant articles (p. 1894). It does not itself estimate a causal relationship between engagement and firm performance.
- **Interpretation boundary:** Use as a theory-development claim. Do not write that community engagement *has been proven* to create sustained competitive advantage without specific empirical evidence.
- **Related project/theory/concept:** Pre-arrival Foundation; RBV; stakeholder theory; corporate-community engagement; CSR; international business; sustainability.
