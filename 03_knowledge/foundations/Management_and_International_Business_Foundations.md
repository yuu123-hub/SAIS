# Management and International Business Foundations

## Status and use

This is a pre-arrival orientation map. It helps you recognise key ideas when reading; it does not prove a claim in an assignment. Verify definitions and debates in the original/current literature before citing them.

## A simple map

```text
Firm resources and capabilities -> strategy -> performance
          |
          +-- stakeholders / legitimacy
          +-- country, institutions, culture, global competition
```

## 1. Resource-based view (RBV)

**白話中文：** 一間公司可能因為擁有競爭者難以取得、模仿或替代的資源與能力而表現較好，例如信任關係、專門知識、品牌聲譽或組織慣例。

**Academic English:** The resource-based view explains sustained advantage through resources and capabilities that are valuable, rare, difficult to imitate and not easily substitutable.

**Use it when:** analysing why one firm may outperform competitors with apparently similar products or markets.

**Do not overclaim:** a resource is not automatically valuable in every context, and RBV can understate industry, institutions and power relations.

**Useful question:** What makes this capability difficult to copy, and what external conditions could weaken its value?

## 2. Stakeholder theory and CSR

**白話中文：** 公司會影響員工、顧客、供應商、社區、政府與環境團體，也會受到這些群體影響。CSR 可以是倫理責任，也可能影響信任、風險、聲譽或資源累積。

**Academic English:** Stakeholder theory considers how organisations create, distribute and justify value across groups that affect or are affected by the organisation.

**Use it when:** evaluating a business decision that creates different consequences for different groups.

**Critical move:** distinguish a normative claim (what a firm ought to do) from an instrumental claim (what may improve performance). Neither follows automatically from the other.

## 3. Strategy and industry structure

**白話中文：** RBV 問公司內部有什麼特別能力；產業分析問公司外部有什麼競爭規則。好的策略分析通常兩者都看。

**Academic English:** Industry analysis examines how competitive forces, entry barriers, substitutes and buyer/supplier power shape the attractiveness of a market.

**Critical move:** avoid treating an industry framework as a list. Explain which force matters, through what mechanism, and why it changes the firm's options.

## 4. Internationalisation and the multinational enterprise

**白話中文：** 海外擴張不是把產品搬到另一個國家而已。公司還要處理制度、文化與地理距離、在地知識、合作夥伴，以及全球整合和在地回應之間的取捨。

**Academic English:** International-business analysis considers how firms balance cross-border integration, local responsiveness, institutional differences and knowledge transfer.

**Useful questions:**

- What country-level institution changes the cost, risk or legitimacy of this decision?
- Is the firm learning gradually, acquiring local knowledge, or transferring an existing capability?
- Does a global standard create efficiency but reduce local fit?

## 5. Dynamic capabilities

**白話中文：** 有資源不代表永遠有優勢。市場或科技改變時，公司還需要發現機會、決定如何回應，以及重新配置資源。

**Academic English:** Dynamic capabilities concern an organisation's ability to sense opportunities and threats, seize opportunities and reconfigure its resource base.

**Critical move:** identify observable actions or processes. Do not use “dynamic capability” merely as a sophisticated synonym for “being good at change”.

## 6. Cross-cultural management

**白話中文：** 國家或群體差異可能影響溝通、階層與領導期待，但文化分數不能代表每一個人的性格或行為。

**Academic English:** Cross-cultural analysis examines patterned differences while avoiding essentialist assumptions that reduce individuals to national averages.

**Critical move:** compare culture with institutional, occupational, organisational and individual explanations.

## Build an argument, not a glossary

1. State a bounded claim.
2. Select the theory that explains the mechanism.
3. Identify evidence that supports and challenges the claim.
4. Name at least one limitation or alternative explanation.
5. Explain the managerial implication with conditions, not certainty.
