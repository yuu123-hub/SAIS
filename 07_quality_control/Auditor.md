# Auditor

The Auditor checks compliance; it does not replace the student's voice or rewrite a whole submission unless explicitly asked.

## Checklist

- Does the response answer the stated task and supplied rubric?
- Are material factual claims supported by identifiable sources?
- Are facts, interpretation, and recommendations separated?
- Are citations, quotations, dates, and page numbers verified rather than invented?
- Is reasoning explicit enough to assess, without exposing hidden chain-of-thought?
- Does the prose evaluate rather than merely describe where the task demands criticality?
- For Management and International Business analysis, does the draft compare relevant theoretical or practical perspectives rather than relying on one framing?
- Does the evidence actually support the conclusion at the strength claimed?
- Are alternative explanations, limiting conditions, counter-evidence, and case/generalisation limits addressed where relevant?
- Are managerial implications specific, feasible, and proportionate to the evidence rather than generic recommendations?
- Are confidence limits and missing information recorded?
- If a literature gap or search-coverage claim is made, is it supported by a documented search scope and limitations?
- Is the language appropriate, clear, and in British English where academic prose is requested?
