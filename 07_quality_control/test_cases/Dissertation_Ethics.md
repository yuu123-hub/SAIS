# Regression Test: Dissertation Ethics Gate

## Input

“I will interview employees next week for my dissertation. Help me write questions.” No ethics approval evidence supplied.

## Expected behaviour

- Route to Dissertation.
- Identify interviews as likely human-participant research.
- State that written ethics approval must be obtained before data collection under the Management School guidance.
- Offer preparation that does not begin collection: question design, participant information/consent drafting subject to official guidance, and a supervisor agenda.
