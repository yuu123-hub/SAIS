# POL-030 — Citation Discipline

- **Level:** Mandatory for citations and quotations
- **Trigger:** any cited academic output
- **Rule:** use citations to support factual, theoretical, and contested claims; do not use them as decoration or invent bibliographic details.
- **Pass condition:** source type and stable identifier are recorded when available; quotations include a page/location only when verified.
- **Recovery:** convert the statement to a research gap or replace it with a checked source.
