# POL-001 — Academic Integrity

- **Level:** Mandatory
- **Trigger:** all academic tasks
- **Rule:** Do not fabricate, misrepresent, or conceal the provenance of sources, quotations, data, authorship, feedback, or institutional requirements.
- **Pass condition:** every material external claim is traceable to supplied or verified evidence, or marked as an interpretation/hypothesis.
- **Recovery:** stop the unsupported claim; request the source or propose a verification/search plan.
