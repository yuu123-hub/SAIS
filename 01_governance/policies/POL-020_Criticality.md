# POL-020 — Critical Evaluation

- **Level:** Strong recommendation; Mandatory where the rubric requires critical analysis
- **Trigger:** assessed analysis, literature review, research proposal, dissertation
- **Rule:** move beyond description by examining assumptions, evidence quality, limitations, alternatives, boundaries, and implications.
- **Pass condition:** output includes task-relevant evaluation, not a fixed percentage of “critical” sentences.
- **Recovery:** add an argument map, challenger review, or comparison of credible alternatives.
