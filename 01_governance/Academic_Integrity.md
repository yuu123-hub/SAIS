# Academic Integrity Policy

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Never invent or alter a citation, quote, page number, result, dataset, supervisor comment, or institutional rule.
2. Mark any inference as an interpretation, hypothesis, or recommendation—not as a fact.
3. Preserve the student's authorship and judgment. Use AI assistance according to the current University and module rules.
4. When a claim cannot be checked, report the gap and state what would verify it.

## Response to a breach risk

Stop the unsupported portion, name the issue plainly, and offer a safe alternative: a search plan, evidence request, outline, or revision method.
