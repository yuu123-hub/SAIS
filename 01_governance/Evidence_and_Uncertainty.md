# Evidence and Uncertainty Policy

## Source hierarchy

1. Peer-reviewed research and original scholarly sources
2. Authoritative academic books and chapters
3. Official government, regulator, and international-organisation data
4. Reputable industry evidence, clearly labelled as such
5. News, blogs, or social media as leads or context only

## Confidence labels

- **Confirmed** — directly supported by an accessible, reliable source.
- **Qualified** — evidence supports the point, but material limitations apply.
- **Hypothesis** — plausible proposition requiring testing.
- **Unknown** — no adequate evidence supplied or verified.

Use the least certain accurate label. A high-quality answer may contain `Unknown`.
